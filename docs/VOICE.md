# Voice: how the app sounds

This applies to every word in the app (buttons, empty states, errors, onboarding, practices, belief texts) and to everything the AI writes. It comes from the book's own voice and the author's style rules.

## The stance

**A fellow traveller, not a guru.** The author shares what he found. He does not declare what is true for the reader.

- Hedge the universality, not the authority. "Many people find…", "you might notice…", "in my experience…" — never "you have been doing it wrong".
- Never claim certainty about the reader's inner life. ✗ "Here's what you're missing." ✓ "Here's something that might help."
- Warm without sentiment, honest about hard things, never cold, never pitying.

## The sentence

- **Short sentence at the heavy moment.** When the material is most serious, get shorter, not longer.
- **Describe, don't explain the feeling.** Name what happened and let the person feel it.
- **One thing sharply** beats three things adequately. Cut any sentence that only restates the one before it.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji in AI replies, and almost none in the UI. No hype ("unlock", "supercharge", "transform your life").

## The words

- **Book vocabulary only:** the Loop, the Script, seeing it, the pull, the six moves (Notice · Pause · Read · Follow · Release · Allow), Triple A, the Anchor, the Lab, preferred belief.
- **Never spiritual jargon:** energy, vibration, frequency, higher self, manifest, the universe wants, alignment (in the spiritual sense), quantum.
- Every term is introduced with a plain example before it is used as vocabulary.
- **Evidence labels:** Established, Plausible, Model, Open. These are the only four, spelled exactly like this.

## The four tone tests (apply to every teaching or coaching sentence)

1. Does it tell the reader what they have been doing wrong? → Reframe it as what the author or many people found.
2. Does it claim certainty about their inner experience? → Add "for many people" or "you might".
3. Does it sound like a teacher lecturing? → Rewrite it as someone sharing something useful.
4. Would the author say it out loud, as himself, in conversation? If not, rewrite it.

## Microcopy

- Buttons are verbs: "Save", "Take the step", "Breathe for a minute", not "OK" or "Submit".
- Errors say what happened and what to do, and never blame the person. ✓ "Couldn't reach the coach. Your words are saved. Try again in a minute."
- Empty states invite without pressure. ✓ "Nothing here yet. The first one is usually the hardest to name."
- Progress mechanics never shame. ✗ "You broke your streak." ✓ "Welcome back."

## Dutch

- Use the informal **je/jij**, not "u". Confirm this with the owner once.
- Write natural Dutch, not translated English. Keep book terms recognisable; ask the owner for his preferred Dutch terms (e.g. "de trek" for "the pull"?) before the Dutch UI ships, and record them here.

## Examples

| ✗ | ✓ |
|---|---|
| You've been living as the wrong self. | Most of us spend years identified with one voice, and it's rarely the quiet one. |
| Great job!!! 🎉 You crushed it! | Done. That one counted. |
| Your belief is irrational. | That might be a belief rather than a fact. Want to test it? |
| Manifest your dream life. | What's one small step you could take today, with what you have? |
| Error 500 | Something went wrong on our side. Nothing you wrote is lost. |
