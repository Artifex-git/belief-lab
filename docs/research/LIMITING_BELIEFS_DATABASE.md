# Limiting Beliefs Database — v0.6 (belief tree)

*5 roots → 37 core beliefs → 529 surface beliefs across 11 life areas. A **model** that groups beliefs which look different but share a core. Sources: CBT core-belief work, schema therapy, the Break the Loop / Follow the Pull book, Bashar's teachings (Blueprint for Change, The Masters of Limitation, online material, the transcript-derived list) translated into secular language, and Richard Koch's books (Unreasonable Success, The 80/20 Principle, Living the 80/20 Way). Each surface belief is tagged as a rule, prediction or attitude.*

New in v0.6: five cores (certain, fused, grief, owe, grind), about 170 new surface beliefs, five existing beliefs moved (O4, O9, U4, U8 → certain; V5-discomfort-14 → fused). Placement test for effort beliefs: hard work makes a result *count* → earn; hard work *produces* the result → grind; enjoyment only *allowed* after hardship → deserve/joy. Existing ids unchanged.

Safety: grief beliefs get gentle copy and a route to help; responsibility is never blame (never offered about abuse or crime); beliefs that may signal low mood ("Nothing really excites me") point to support, not harder coaching.

## Root: Worth

*Preferred:* I'm enough to begin, and I can grow from here.

*What it costs:* never starting, or never feeling finished  
*Rules it runs:* If I'm not the best, I'm nothing special. · I should be further along by now.

### I have to earn my worth.
*Preferred:* My worth is a given; what I do is how I express it.  
*Protects you from:* being judged as lazy or useless  
*What it costs:* rest that never comes, and a worth that resets every day  
*Rules it runs:* If I'm not productive, I'm lazy. · If I rest, I fall behind.

- **Self** · I have to earn the right to take up space. → *My presence doesn't require a receipt.* `rule` `W2`
- **Work** · If I stop achieving, I stop mattering. → *What I do adds to my life. It was never the source of my worth.* `rule` `W4`
- **Work** · I am only as valuable as my last result. → *My last result was one thing I did. It's not a referendum on me.* `attitude` `A1`
- **Work** · Rest has to be earned by output. → *Rest is part of the work, not a reward that comes after it.* `rule` `A2`
- **Work** · If I'm not the best, I've failed. → *Getting better than I was is its own complete win.* `rule` `A3`
- **Work** · My worth is measured by what I've built, not who I am. → *What I've built came from who I already am — not the other way around.* `attitude` `A5`
- **Work** · If it comes easily, it doesn't really count. → *Something being easy for me doesn't make it worth less.* `rule` `A6`
- **Work** · I have to prove myself again with every new person or room. → *What I've already proven doesn't reset every time the audience changes.* `rule` `A9`
- **Work** · Ambition and contentment can't coexist. → *I can want more and be genuinely glad about where I already am.* `attitude` `A10`
- **Work** · Success I didn't struggle for isn't really mine. → *Ease doesn't disqualify something from being genuinely mine.* `attitude` `G8`
- **Work** · If it didn't require struggle, it isn't a real accomplishment. → *Ease doesn't disqualify an accomplishment from being real.* `rule` `T7`
- **Love** · I have to keep giving to be worth keeping. → *I can be kept for who I am, not only for what I give.* `rule` `N-earn-1`
- **Body and health** · I only deserve rest after a productive day. → *Rest is part of how I work, not a reward for it.* `attitude` `N-earn-2`

- **Rest and time** · A day off has to be productive to count. → *A day off counts by being off.* `rule` `V5-earn-1`
- **Body and health** · I have to deserve food before I can enjoy it. → *Eating is care, not a reward.* `rule` `V5-earn-26`
- **Family** · A good parent puts themselves last. → *A rested parent is a better parent.* `attitude` `V5-earn-35`

- **Body and health** · Sleep is wasted time I could be using. → *Sleep is how my mind and body get ready for what's next.* `attitude` `V6-earn-14` · Masters ch.10

### My needs are a burden.

## Root: Belonging

*What it costs:* love that only ever reaches the edited version  
*Rules it runs:* If they really knew me, they'd go. · I should show only the parts people like.

- **Love** · Love has to be earned and can be taken away at any moment. → *Real love doesn't need to be re-earned every day.* `rule` `N-unlovable-1`
- **Family** · I was loved for being good, not for being me. → *I can learn to love the parts of me that weren't praised.* `attitude` `N-unlovable-2`
