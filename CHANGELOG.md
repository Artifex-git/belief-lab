# Changelog

Lines under a version are shown to testers in the app under "What's new". Write them in plain language. Add `(#12)` when a line came from a feedback issue.

## Unreleased
- Eleven more exercises from the book, in its own steps: The Three Breaths, Relax the Judgment, Trace the Reel, Don't wait for the echo, Own it don't blame it, Say what's real, The three questions, The already-have reframe, Let it be all right, Drop the qualifier, Don't sign it.
- Voice on iPhone: keeps listening after each phrase instead of stopping, and says what to switch on (Dictation, microphone permission) when the phone blocks it.
- New practices from the book: The Loop Audit (Appendix B) and Is this a pull? (Appendix C). Both are diagnostics, not tests.
- Voice: recording no longer starts inside the Claude viewer, where it was blocked and could hang the page. It also stops after two minutes or when you leave the app, and writes to the entry less often while you speak.
- Accessibility: fixed heading order, tab roles and page landmarks found by an automated audit (axe) in light and dark. Bottom bar and Practice rows no longer run off screen at large text.
- New: search on the Journey screen finds words across your beliefs, steps, mornings, talks, practices and tests. It stays on your device.
- The app now asks for an invite code the first time it opens on a device. Feedback and error reports need the code too.
- New: a weekly check on Today, "Which belief cost you the most this week?", that opens the Lab on it. Skip any week without a word said about it.
- Journey now notices when one root keeps coming back in your recent entries, and links to it in the belief tree.
- Practice shows the book's six moves, and tags exercises with the move they train.
- New: The morning practice from the book (Appendix I), under Practice. It opens with the breathing safety note, then arrive, breathe, move and set the day.
- New: The Anchor, the book's page for low days. Five short steps, an "I'm in a dip" button on Today, and on days you check in at 3 or lower the app offers the Anchor and keeps the Lab out of reach.

## 0.1.0
- The Belief Lab now installs on your iPhone home screen and opens full-screen.
- A Feedback button on every screen: tell us what broke, what confused you, an idea, or what you love. It takes about 15 seconds.
- The coach works again with an invite code.
- You can import a backup from the claude.ai version (You, Import data).
- Backups now open the iPhone share sheet, so you can save them to Files or send them to yourself.
